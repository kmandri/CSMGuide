Overview

What is PDU?

PagerDuty University delivers role-based enablement for customers across incident response, operations maturity, and digital operations best practices.

How to Engage PDU

When to Bring PDU In

  • New logos or expansions needing structured onboarding
  • Low adoption or maturity gaps surfaced in QBRs
  • Leadership asking for training or standardization
  • Pre-renewal risk mitigation via best-practice uplift

How It Works

  1. Share customer goals, audiences, and timing in Slack or with the PDU team
  2. PDU scopes and confirms delivery plan
  3. Customer comms + calendar sent; materials shared
  4. Delivery + outcomes recap back to CSM
